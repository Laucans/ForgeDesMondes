Design options · DM view density

DM view density C — Roomy

The DM view at 1280 × 800, laid out as the brief of DESIGN-DIRECTION.md (§ “DM view”) says: the live strip, the tab bar, the elements column open on the left, the sheet of Morlox (fixture content) in the centre, and the suggestions pill and the microphone fixed at the bottom right (R98). Only the density differs between the three options.

Trade-offs

  • The easiest to read at a glance: the blocks are clearly separated, a row is a comfortable tap target (40 px, with a gap between rows) and nothing is dense.
  • Shows the least: the sheet down to Comportement (the Histoire block is cut by the bottom edge) and the first two scenes, so the DM scrolls most.
  • The same 320 px open card covers the same area of a window that shows less, so a larger share of what is shown sits next to the pill.
What changes
Every gap inside the panels one step over the standard (§4: card --space-4, panel and group --space-5); reading stays at 1.25 rem, since the next step of the scale (2 rem) is a title’s.
What does not
Regions, colours, states and destructive treatment; no text under 14 px; no control under 24 × 24 px (§9). The 440 px reserve under the sheet is the same in the three, so the end of the sheet clears the pill.
The pill
position: fixed, right and bottom at --space-3, open card capped at 40 % of the window (320 px), over the content and out of the grid. The statblock and the lower right of the sheet pass under it, which R98 allows.

Visible without scrolling (a 1280 × 800 window)

  • Always: the live strip (title, microphone sentence, scene stepper, combat turn, what the players see, Séance menu) and the tab bar, ending at 111 px of the 800.
  • Sheet: the title row, Description, Comportement, the statblock’s head; Histoire cut by the bottom edge.
  • Statblock: CA, PV, Vitesse, the six scores and 0 of its 3 features are clear of the pill (the first starts above it and is cut by it); 3 features are under it (they come out from under it as the sheet scrolls).
  • Elements column: 7 of 11 rows in full, scene summaries visible: Scène 1, Scène 2; the current scene (Scène 3) is cut by the bottom edge, one scroll of the column away.
  • Pill: the open card and the microphone cover the lower right of the window, 320 px wide from y = 407 px, over the sheet’s right column; the sheet’s last line clears it at the end of the scroll (reserve).

Morlox

Humanoïde (gobelinoïde) de taille P, neutre · PNJ

Description

Gobelin marchand du marché noir de Valombre. Rusé, couard, d’une politesse excessive ; il vend tout ce qui brille et rachète ce qui a brûlé.

Comportement

Parle vite, se frotte les mains, recule d’un pas quand on hausse le ton. Ment par réflexe, mais dit la vérité si on lui promet une protection. Siffle ses « s » : « Ssseigneur ». Depuis qu’il a trahi le groupe, évite le regard de Kael et propose des rabais sans qu’on les demande.

Histoire

Chassé de sa tribu pour avoir vendu la réserve de champignons du chef, Morlox s’est installé sous le pont de Valombre il y a six ans. Il y a bâti un petit marché noir où passent les objets tirés des ruines de la Tour d’Ébène. Depuis l’incendie du moulin, il a peur : il sait qui a payé les incendiaires.

Notes du DM

Doit 40 po à Vesna. Connaît le passage sous le moulin. A vendu aux bandits le plan du gué contre l’effacement de sa dette.

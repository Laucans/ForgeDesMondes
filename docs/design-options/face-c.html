Design options · Display face

Display face C — System sans, bold

The face of the display and title steps (§2.1, §9), drawn in sentence case on the product’s own content, with today’s Impact stack beside it.

Trade-offs

  • Zero cost: nothing to host, nothing to load, identical to the body face, so the product has one typeface and hierarchy rests on size and weight alone, as the direction states (§1).
  • The look follows the platform: a DM on Windows and a player on a phone see two different titles, and the cover title has no character of its own.
  • The widest of the three at display weight 700: a long campaign title wraps earlier than with Impact.
Stack
--font-family-heading: system-ui, -apple-system, 'Segoe UI', Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif
Licence
No licence to carry: the face is the one of the visitor’s operating system (San Francisco on macOS, Segoe UI on Windows, Roboto on Android). Nothing is redistributed.
Loading
System font: no file, no request, no flash of a fallback. The drawing below is rendered with this machine’s system face and looks different on another platform.
Weights
700 for display and title (the direction’s only two uses); 400 and 600 appear below only to draw the whole scale in the face.

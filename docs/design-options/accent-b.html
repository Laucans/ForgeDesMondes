Design options · Accent colour

Accent B — Rose

The accent for the primary action, a new value for --color-primary. Drawn on the product’s own screen, campagnes (mock/pages/campagnes.html), with its fixture content.

Trade-offs

  • Sits beside the violet of the surfaces and of the pending state rather than against it: a calmer screen, and a clear break from the old orange-and-amber look.
  • Its hue (about 331°) is 28° from the danger red (359°). The danger button stays outlined (§7.2) and the dialog confirm is the only red fill, so the two never share a fill.
  • Artwork that used amber stays gold (--color-art-gold); no orange is left in the interface, so the change shows on every screen at once.
Value
--color-primary: #f0609f (hover fill #ff6cb2); text on it --color-primary-contrast #14081f
Thresholds
One filled element per view (§3.1). Fill ≥ 3:1 on both dark surfaces, its text ≥ 4.5:1, the focus ring ≥ 3:1 (§3.1, §5, §8.1).
